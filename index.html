About

Why this club exists

The Energy Efficiency Club promotes responsible use of electrical energy and sustainable engineering practice. We analyse consumption, cut losses, and build working solutions that make conservation and green technology visible on campus — not just talked about.

Vision

A campus and society that practises efficient, responsible, sustainable use of electrical energy — through awareness, innovation and technology.

Mission

Promote awareness, innovation and practical implementation of energy-efficient, sustainable technologies among students and society.

  • Promote efficient, responsible use of electrical energy
  • Build awareness of polluting vs. non-polluting devices
  • Analyse and compare appliance energy consumption
  • Push adoption of renewable energy solutions
  • Cut energy losses in everyday campus life

Bigger picture

Lining up with the UN SDGs

Every audit and build we run feeds directly into two global goals:

SDG 7 — Affordable & Clean Energy

Our audits and renewable-tech pushes work toward reliable, clean energy access for everyone on campus.

SDG 13 — Climate Action

Cutting energy loss and carbon footprint, one habit and one appliance at a time.

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption

"Junk to Energy" and our upcycling drives turn waste into working, useful devices.
